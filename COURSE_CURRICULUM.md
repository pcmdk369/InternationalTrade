# International Trade Coordinator — Complete Curriculum

**Level:** Intermediate to Advanced  
**Duration:** 24 weeks (suggested)  
**Study load:** 60–90 minutes per day  
**Languages:** English, Hindi and Thai

## Program design

The 24 modules are organized into six phases. Each module must be expanded into complete textbook lessons and applied work; a module title or outline alone does not count as completed course material.

| Phase | Weeks | Modules | Focus |
|---|---:|---|---|
| I | 1–4 | ITC-01–04 | Trade foundations and ecosystem |
| II | 5–8 | ITC-05–08 | Documents, customs and compliance |
| III | 9–12 | ITC-09–12 | Logistics and supply chain |
| IV | 13–16 | ITC-13–16 | Incoterms, payments and contracts |
| V | 17–20 | ITC-17–20 | Procurement, risk and communication |
| VI | 21–24 | ITC-21–24 | Project management, systems and capstone |

## Phase I — International Trade Foundations

### ITC-01 — International Trade Fundamentals
- Meaning, scope and characteristics of international trade
- Domestic trade versus international trade
- Imports, exports, re-exports and transit trade
- Trade drivers, benefits, limitations and risks
- Basic transaction lifecycle
- Trade terminology and stakeholder map

### ITC-02 — Global Trade Ecosystem
- Buyers, sellers, manufacturers, distributors and agents
- Trading companies and intermediaries
- Banks, insurers, carriers, ports, customs and brokers
- Chambers, regulators and international institutions
- B2B, B2C, D2C and marketplace models
- Roles, authority boundaries and responsibility matrix

### ITC-03 — Import & Export Operations
- Inquiry-to-order workflow
- RFQ, quotation, negotiation and order confirmation
- Purchase orders and sales contracts
- Production, inspection, dispatch and delivery
- Transaction files, milestones and handovers
- Order closure and record retention

### ITC-04 — International Trade Terminology
- Commercial, logistics, customs and finance vocabulary
- Abbreviations and document names
- English-Hindi-Thai glossary
- Professional email and telephone language
- Reading quotations and shipping updates

## Phase II — Documentation & Compliance

### ITC-05 — Commercial Documentation
- Purchase order, proforma and commercial invoices
- Packing list and sales contract
- Certificates of origin and supporting certificates
- Transport and insurance documents
- Document consistency checks and version control

### ITC-06 — Customs & Border Procedures
- Customs roles and clearance stages
- Import/export declarations
- Customs valuation concepts
- Duties, taxes, fees and release processes
- Broker coordination and document readiness

### ITC-07 — HS Code & Product Classification
- Harmonized System structure and interpretation
- Product description, composition, function and condition
- General Rules for Interpretation (GRIs)
- National tariff extensions and binding rulings
- Classification research and escalation
- Never rely on an unverified AI or supplier code

### ITC-08 — Trade Compliance & Regulations
- Import/export controls and permits
- Product standards, labeling and safety requirements
- Sanctions and restricted-party screening
- Origin rules and preferential trade
- Recordkeeping, audit trails and escalation

## Phase III — Logistics & Supply Chain

### ITC-09 — International Logistics
- Supply chain and logistics principles
- Route planning and lead times
- Cargo readiness and handover
- Milestones, visibility and exception management

### ITC-10 — Freight Forwarding
- Forwarder roles and service scope
- RFQ and freight quote comparison
- Origin/destination charges and surcharges
- Booking instructions and shipping instructions
- Forwarder performance and claims coordination

### ITC-11 — Shipping & Transportation
- Sea, air, road, rail and multimodal freight
- FCL/LCL, container types and cargo suitability
- B/L, sea waybill and AWB concepts
- ETD, ETA, cut-offs, transshipment and tracking
- Dangerous goods awareness and specialist handling

### ITC-12 — Warehouse & Inventory
- Receiving, put-away, storage and dispatch
- SKU, batch, lot and serial control
- FIFO/FEFO and stock accuracy
- Cycle counts, discrepancies and damage reports
- Proof of delivery and reverse logistics

## Phase IV — Commercial Operations

### ITC-13 — Incoterms® 2020
- Purpose, scope and correct rule selection
- All 11 rules and mode suitability
- Delivery, risk, costs and named places
- Seller/buyer obligations and practical scenarios
- Correct quotation wording
- Ownership and payment are separate contractual matters

### ITC-14 — International Payments
- Advance payment, open account and documentary collection
- Documentary credits / letters of credit
- Bank transfer and payment evidence
- Payment milestones and reconciliation
- Fraud controls and independent bank-detail verification

### ITC-15 — Foreign Exchange & Trade Finance
- Exchange rates, spreads and conversion
- Currency exposure and hedging concepts
- Working capital and cash-flow timing
- Trade finance instruments and bank roles
- Landed cost, margin and sensitivity analysis

### ITC-16 — Contracts & Negotiation
- Contract formation and essential clauses
- Product specs, quantity, price and tolerances
- Delivery, inspection, warranties and claims
- Force majeure, dispute resolution and governing law
- Negotiation preparation and approval limits

## Phase V — Advanced Trade Management

### ITC-17 — International Procurement
- Demand planning and purchase requisitions
- RFQ design and bid evaluation
- Total cost of ownership
- Purchase order controls and supplier onboarding

### ITC-18 — Sourcing & Supplier Management
- Supplier discovery and due diligence
- Capability, capacity and quality evaluation
- Audits, samples and inspections
- Scorecards, corrective action and continuity

### ITC-19 — Trade Risk Management
- Commercial, operational, financial and country risks
- Fraud, counterfeit and cargo risks
- Risk registers, controls and escalation
- Business continuity and incident response

### ITC-20 — International Business Communication
- Professional email, calls and meetings
- Cross-cultural communication
- Negotiation and conflict de-escalation
- English-Hindi-Thai workplace practice
- Clear status updates and action ownership

## Phase VI — Professional Coordination & Technology

### ITC-21 — Trade Project Management
- Work breakdown, RACI and milestones
- Critical path and deadline control
- Multi-order coordination and handovers
- Issue, change and decision logs

### ITC-22 — ERP, CRM & Trade Software
- Excel and Google Sheets
- Odoo/SAP ERP concepts
- CRM pipeline and contact records
- Document management and collaboration
- Data quality, access control and auditability

### ITC-23 — Trade Analytics & Reporting
- Operational KPIs
- PivotTables, Power Query and dashboards
- Cost variance, on-time delivery and cycle time
- Daily, weekly and monthly reporting
- Data interpretation and management recommendations

### ITC-24 — Professional Trade Simulation
- End-to-end simulated import/export transaction
- Commercial negotiation and approvals
- Document pack and compliance checks
- Shipment execution and exception handling
- Delivery, reconciliation and closure report
- Portfolio presentation and final examination

## Assessment model

| Component | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Theory and terminology | 15% |
| Documentation practical | 20% |
| Logistics coordination | 15% |
| Commercial and financial calculations | 15% |
| Communication and negotiation | 10% |
| Software and reporting | 10% |
| Capstone transaction | 15% |
| **Total** | **100%** |

Suggested pass mark: 70%. Critical compliance, document-integrity and fraud-control failures must be remediated regardless of aggregate score.

## Required portfolio

- Trade glossary (minimum 150 terms)
- Supplier evaluation and RFQ comparison
- Buyer quotation and PO
- Commercial invoice and packing list
- Shipment tracker and freight comparison
- Customs document readiness checklist
- Landed-cost calculator
- Risk register and SOP
- Daily and weekly management reports
- Final simulated transaction file

## Currency, law and standards note

Use Incoterms® 2020 as the named ICC edition while checking whether later ICC publications or amendments have been issued before real-world use. Customs tariff schedules, tax rates, product controls, sanctions and national requirements change. Learners must verify current official sources for the relevant origin, destination, product and date.
