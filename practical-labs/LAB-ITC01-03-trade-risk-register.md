# LAB-ITC01-03 — Build a Trade Risk Register

**Level:** Intermediate | **Time:** 75 minutes | **Deliverable:** Risk register and action plan

## Objective
Convert vague concerns into actionable risk statements with owners, controls, triggers and evidence.

## Risk statement format
Because **[cause]**, **[uncertain event]** may occur, resulting in **[consequence]**.

## Scoring method
Likelihood and impact are scored 1–5. Inherent score = L × I. Example bands: 1–4 low, 5–9 moderate, 10–16 high, 17–25 critical. Your organisation must approve its own definitions. Legal prohibitions, safety stops and sanctions controls cannot be overridden by a score.

## Required scenarios
1. Supplier legal identity is not independently verified.
2. Invoice model number differs from approved PO.
3. Import permit applicability is uncertain.
4. Bank details change by email shortly before payment.
5. Shipment ETA slips beyond the buyer's production buffer.
6. Cargo arrives wet or visibly damaged.

For each, describe immediate containment, accountable owner, specialist to consult, evidence to preserve and closure criteria.

## Model control examples
- Identity risk: verify through independent company registry and previously verified contact; retain dated evidence.
- Document mismatch: stop affected approval/declaration, reconcile against approved PO and obtain authorised corrected documents.
- Permit uncertainty: hold shipment commitment/release and ask competent authority or qualified broker.
- Bank-change fraud: hold payment; verify via known telephone number and dual approval; alert finance/security.
- Delay: obtain carrier evidence, assess downstream impact, communicate revised ETA and mitigation.
- Damage: photograph before unpacking where safe, note exceptions on receipt, notify carrier/insurer within policy/contract deadlines.

## Marking rubric
A complete submission has at least six well-formed risks, credible scores, preventive and detective controls, named owners, measurable triggers and traceable evidence. Avoid generic controls such as “be careful”.
