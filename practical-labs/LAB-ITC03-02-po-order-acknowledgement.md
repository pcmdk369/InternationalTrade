# LAB-ITC03-02 — Purchase Order and Order Acknowledgement Control

**Time:** 75–90 minutes | **Deliverable:** PO control pack and discrepancy log

## Scenario
Approved order: 240 units, Model MX-240A, USD 32/unit, delivery to the buyer's named warehouse under the agreed delivery rule and edition, cargo ready by 15 March. Supplier acknowledgement states 200 units, Model MX-240B, USD 34/unit, cargo ready 29 March.

## Tasks
1. Draft the PO field list and approval record.
2. Compare each acknowledgement field with the PO.
3. Identify all discrepancies and their cost/schedule/technical impacts.
4. Prepare a hold/escalation notice requesting written clarification.
5. Draft a change request form; do not approve it.
6. Show how revised PO versions and acknowledgement evidence will be stored.

## Discrepancy log
| Field | Approved PO | Supplier acknowledgement | Impact | Owner | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quantity | 240 | 200 | Short quantity | Buyer | Hold |
| Model | MX-240A | MX-240B | Technical mismatch | Engineering | Hold |
| Unit price | USD 32 | USD 34 | Cost increase | Procurement/finance | Approval required |
| Cargo-ready | 15 March | 29 March | 14-day delay | Operations | Re-plan/approval |

## Model answer
Do not treat the acknowledgement as matching acceptance. Keep the order in exception status. Obtain technical confirmation for model substitution, commercial approval for price/quantity changes, schedule impact review and a formally revised PO plus supplier confirmation before execution.
