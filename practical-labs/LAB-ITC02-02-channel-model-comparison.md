# LAB-ITC02-02 — Compare B2B, B2C, D2C and Marketplace Channels

**Level:** Intermediate | **Time:** 75–90 minutes

## Scenario
A Thai business imports reusable insulated bottles. Management is considering: (A) wholesale sales to retailers, (B) direct retail sales, (C) its own D2C website, and (D) third-party marketplace listings.

## Task 1 — Complete the channel matrix
For each channel, identify:
- Customer and contracting seller.
- Invoice issuer and payment recipient.
- Importer of record and customs-data owner.
- Fulfilment and last-mile provider.
- Returns, warranty and complaint owner.
- Product labelling and consumer-law questions.
- Platform/payment fees, fulfilment cost and return allowance.
- Data/privacy responsibility.
- Revenue, variable cost and contribution per order.

## Task 2 — Unit economics
Use this training example (currency units are illustrative):
- Selling price: 500
- Product cost: 180
- Freight/fulfilment: 55
- Payment/platform fee: 25
- Returns allowance: 20
- Other variable cost: 15

Contribution before overhead = 500 − 180 − 55 − 25 − 20 − 15 = **205**.

If marketplace fee rises from 25 to 40, contribution falls to **190**. State which costs are fixed, variable or volume-dependent; do not treat tax collected on behalf of government as revenue.

## Task 3 — Recommendation
Recommend a channel only after comparing contribution, working capital, customer access, compliance burden, service capability and risk. Explain assumptions and missing data.

## Marking
Full credit requires clear seller/importer identification, realistic cost categories, consumer compliance questions and transparent assumptions. A channel must not be selected solely on gross sales.
