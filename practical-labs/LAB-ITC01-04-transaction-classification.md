# LAB-ITC01-04 — Classify Trade and Cargo Movements

**Level:** Foundation / Intermediate | **Time:** 45–60 minutes

## Instructions
For each scenario, state the likely commercial/customs movement description and list what additional facts are needed. Do not infer a customs procedure from the invoice alone.

## Scenarios
1. A buyer in Bangkok buys stock from a supplier in Chiang Mai.
2. A Thai company buys goods from Japan and brings them into Thailand.
3. A Thai manufacturer ships finished goods to a customer in Malaysia.
4. Goods are moved through a third territory under a formal customs transit procedure.
5. Cargo is unloaded at a hub and loaded onto a connecting vessel.
6. Goods previously imported into a country are later sent abroad.
7. A trader in Thailand buys from Country A and sells to Country B; the goods ship directly from A to B and never enter Thailand.
8. Goods enter a country temporarily for an exhibition and are intended to leave again.

## Answer guide
1. Domestic trade.
2. Import into Thailand (and export from the origin customs territory).
3. Export from Thailand (and import in destination).
4. Transit in the intermediate territory, subject to its procedure.
5. Transshipment as a logistics event; customs status must be checked separately.
6. Potential re-export/return procedure; verify original customs regime and local rules.
7. Potential third-country trade/merchanting; determine ownership, invoicing, statistical reporting and customs treatment in the countries where goods move.
8. Potential temporary admission procedure; check conditions, time limit, guarantee and re-export evidence.

## Required follow-up questions
For every case, identify:
- Physical ship-from and final destination.
- Countries/customs territories crossed.
- Ownership transfer points.
- Customs procedure in each territory.
- Product controls and permits.
- Responsible declarant and evidence.

## Assessment
Full credit requires distinguishing physical movement, commercial sale and customs procedure. Answers such as “international” without identifying import/export/transit context are incomplete.
