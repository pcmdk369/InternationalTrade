# LAB-ITC03-01 — RFQ and Supplier Quotation Comparison

**Time:** 90–120 minutes | **Deliverable:** RFQ + clarification log + comparison memo

## Scenario
A Thai company needs 100 industrial fans. Technical details are incomplete. Three suppliers have asked for an RFQ.

## Part A — Clarify
Prepare questions for airflow, pressure, motor power, voltage/frequency, materials, dimensions, noise, mounting, operating environment, safety certification, test standard, warranty, spare parts, packaging and destination. Mark engineering approval items; do not invent specifications.

## Part B — Issue RFQ
Include item code, specification revision, quantity/unit, required date, destination, proposed delivery rule, currency, payment proposal, quality/inspection, documents, quote validity and response deadline.

## Part C — Compare
Use a common currency basis and record exchange-rate source/date. Compare product conformity first, then normalise price, packaging, freight, insurance, destination charges, lead time, warranty and payment terms. Label unverified duties/taxes as estimates and seek qualified confirmation.

## Part D — Recommendation
Provide mandatory pass/fail gates, weighted score, deviations, assumptions, risks and approval required.

## Model review checklist
- Same revision and unit across all bids.
- All exclusions identified.
- Currency conversion traceable.
- Delivery term includes named place and edition.
- Compliance and capacity checked.
- Lowest unit price not automatically selected.
