# LAB-ITC01-02 — Stakeholder Mapping and RACI

**Level:** Intermediate | **Time:** 90 minutes | **Deliverable:** Stakeholder register + RACI matrix

## Scenario
A Thai importer purchases 250 industrial water pumps from an overseas manufacturer. The seller appoints a freight forwarder; the buyer uses an authorised customs broker. Cargo moves by sea and is delivered to the buyer's warehouse.

## Part A — Stakeholder register
Create one row for every party. Required fields: legal name; country; role; contract with whom; decision authority; operational contact; verified contact channel; information held; risk/escalation owner.

Include buyer/importer, seller/exporter, manufacturer (if different), freight forwarder, ocean carrier, customs broker, insurer, bank/payment provider, inspection body, port/terminal, warehouse and competent authorities.

## Part B — RACI
Use R = Responsible, A = Accountable, C = Consulted, I = Informed. Assign one A per task.

| Activity | Buyer | Seller | Forwarder | Carrier | Broker | Coordinator |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Approve technical specification | A | R | I | I | C | R (document control) |
| Confirm cargo-ready date | C | A/R | I | I | I | R (tracking) |
| Arrange booking | Depends on contract | Depends on contract | R if appointed | R for acceptance | I | R (coordination) |
| Prepare export documents | C | A/R or assigned exporter | C | C | C | R (collect/review) |
| Submit import declaration | A (importer) | I | C | I | R if authorised | R (follow-up) |
| Confirm delivery receipt | A/R | I | C | C | I | R (obtain evidence) |

This is a training example. Replace assignments with actual contract, Incoterms® rule and local authorisations.

## Part C — Escalation
For each issue, name the first owner and escalation path:
1. Product model differs from PO.
2. Carrier changes ETA.
3. Broker requests missing product composition.
4. Suspected payment instruction fraud.
5. Cargo arrives damaged.

## Submission standard
- Every party has a defined role and verified contact.
- One accountable owner per activity.
- RACI reflects actual contractual allocation.
- Critical legal, product-safety and fraud issues are escalated, not decided by an unqualified coordinator.

## Model review points
The importer remains responsible for accurate information and its legal obligations even when a broker is appointed. A forwarder may act as agent or principal; confirm the service contract. Carrier, consignee and notify party are distinct roles. Never assume a role from a person's email signature alone.
