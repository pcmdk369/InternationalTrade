# LAB-ITC01-01 — Build an International Trade Transaction Map

**Related lesson:** ITC-01-LES-01  
**Level:** Intermediate foundation  
**Estimated time:** 60 minutes  
**Scenario:** Fictional Thailand–China machinery transaction

## Scenario brief

Bangkok Example Manufacturing Co. (Thailand) wants to purchase 1,000 industrial pump units from Shenzhen Example Machinery Co. (China). The buyer requests delivery to its Bangkok warehouse. No real supplier, price, HS code, duty rate or permit is represented in this exercise.
