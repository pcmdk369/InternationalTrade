# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve the PCMDK369™ International Trade Academy.

## Before editing

1. Read `SKILL.md` and `COURSE_CURRICULUM.md`.
2. Check the relevant course index and roadmap.
3. Search for an existing lesson or template before creating a duplicate.
4. Preserve stable course and lesson IDs.

## Content requirements

- Write original, substantial teaching material.
- Use English trade terms consistently, with Hindi and Thai explanations.
- Separate law, contract, industry practice and company policy.
- Cite authoritative sources for regulatory, customs and standards claims.
- Mark hypothetical data as fictional.
- Never publish personal data, real bank details or confidential commercial records.
- Do not invent HS codes, tariffs, permits, certificates or government forms.
- Do not present AI output as a legal or customs determination.

## File naming

- Courses: `course-types/courses/ITC-XX/`
- Lessons: `ITC-XX-LES-YY-short-title.md`
- Labs: `LAB-ITCXX-YY-short-title.md`
- SOPs: `SOP-ITC-###-short-title.md`

## Review checklist

- [ ] Learning outcomes are measurable.
- [ ] Definitions are accurate and sufficiently explained.
- [ ] English, Hindi and Thai terms are consistent.
- [ ] Examples and calculations are checked.
- [ ] Responsibilities and escalation points are clear.
- [ ] Compliance caveats are appropriate.
- [ ] Exercises have deliverables and grading criteria.
- [ ] References are authoritative and current enough.
- [ ] Internal links work.
- [ ] Roadmap and course index reflect actual status.

## Completion status

Use Planned, In Progress, Review or Complete. A course is not Complete until all required lessons, practical work, assessments, answer keys/rubrics and quality checks are present.
