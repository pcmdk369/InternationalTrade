# Import / Export Transaction Lifecycle

## Purpose

Provide a controlled checklist for coordinating a simulated or authorized trade transaction. Adapt it to the contract, product, origin, destination, transport mode and company approval matrix.

## Workflow and gates

| Stage | Coordinator action | Evidence / output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Inquiry | Record buyer, seller, product, quantity, destination and date | Inquiry record | Required information complete |
| 2. Due diligence | Route buyer/supplier checks to authorized teams | Verification record | Approval to proceed |
| 3. Product review | Obtain specifications, intended use and regulatory information | Product data sheet | Compliance review as needed |
| 4. RFQ | Request comparable supplier and freight quotations | RFQ and comparison | Commercial review |
| 5. Offer | Prepare quotation with currency, validity, delivery rule and assumptions | Approved quotation | Authorized approval |
| 6. Contract | Track agreed terms and amendments | PO / contract | Authorized sign-off |
| 7. Payment | Coordinate agreed milestones and bank evidence | Payment status record | Finance/bank confirmation |
| 8. Production | Monitor readiness and inspection milestones | Production update | Quality release |
| 9. Booking | Coordinate booking, cargo details and cut-offs | Booking confirmation | Cargo and document readiness |
| 10. Documents | Cross-check names, quantities, weights, packages and references | Document checklist | Discrepancies resolved |
| 11. Export | Coordinate exporter and broker requirements | Export status evidence | Export release as applicable |
| 12. Transit | Monitor carrier milestones and exceptions | Tracking record | Exception escalation |
| 13. Import | Coordinate importer and destination broker requirements | Import status evidence | Customs release |
| 14. Delivery | Arrange receiving, delivery appointment and proof | POD / receipt | Delivery confirmed |
| 15. Closure | Reconcile cost, payment, claims and archive | Closure report | Manager sign-off |

## Mandatory controls

- Use a unique order ID and shipment ID.
- Record the owner and due date for every open action.
- Verify all amendments against authorized instructions.
- Independently verify bank-account changes through a trusted channel.
- Never alter or fabricate customs, origin, inspection or transport evidence.
- Escalate uncertain HS classification, licensing, sanctions, valuation and tax issues.
- Protect confidential buyer, supplier, pricing and shipment information.
- Retain records according to applicable law and company policy.
