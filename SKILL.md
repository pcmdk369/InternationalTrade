# International Trade Academy — Learning & Development Skill

## Mission

Develop a complete, accurate and practical International Trade Coordinator learning repository from intermediate to advanced level. Treat this as a textbook and workplace training system, not a list of course titles.

## Teaching languages

- English: authoritative professional terminology and document wording.
- Hindi: accessible conceptual explanations and examples.
- Thai: practical workplace vocabulary and phrases, with pronunciation/transliteration where useful.

Do not translate legal document titles in a way that obscures the original English term. Introduce the English term first, then explain it in Hindi and Thai.

## Required lesson structure

Every substantive lesson should include:

1. Course ID, lesson ID, title, level and estimated study time.
2. Learning objectives with measurable verbs.
3. Prerequisites and required materials.
4. Key terminology table (English / Hindi / Thai / explanation).
5. Formal English definition.
6. Detailed Hindi explanation, without skipping fundamentals.
7. Thai workplace equivalent and usage notes.
8. Scope, purpose, participants and responsibility boundaries.
9. Step-by-step operational workflow.
10. Worked example with assumptions and calculations where relevant.
11. Practical workplace scenario or case study.
12. Common mistakes, controls and escalation points.
13. Practical assignment with deliverables.
14. Knowledge check and answer key or grading rubric.
15. References and a date-sensitive verification note.

## Academic accuracy rules

- Distinguish legal requirements, contractual terms, industry practice and internal company policy.
- Do not invent laws, tariff rates, HS codes, forms, certificate requirements or government procedures.
- Explain jurisdiction and product dependency.
- State assumptions for numerical examples.
- Use illustrative data clearly marked as fictional.
- Incoterms® 2020 rules allocate defined delivery obligations, costs and risk; they do not by themselves govern title/ownership or payment.
- Do not provide a definitive customs classification without adequate product facts and authoritative tariff verification.
- Distinguish customs value, transaction price, landed cost, tax base and accounting cost.
- Never advise falsifying, backdating or misrepresenting trade documents.
- Encourage official authority, licensed broker, bank, insurer or legal professional review when required.

## Practical training standard

Practical tasks should produce reusable artifacts: spreadsheets, checklists, document drafts, registers, dashboards, SOPs, email drafts or decision logs. Include expected columns, sample rows and validation rules. Avoid using real personal or bank data.

## Repository conventions

- Use Markdown for lessons and documentation.
- Use stable IDs: ITC-01, LES-01, LAB-01, SOP-ITC-001.
- Keep one lesson per file when lessons become substantial.
- Keep answer keys separate when appropriate.
- Link related lessons, templates, labs and references.
- Update course index and progress/roadmap when adding content.
- Do not mark an entire course complete when only its outline exists.
- Preserve existing user content; inspect before replacing.
- Use descriptive filenames and relative links.
- Use tables for compact comparisons, not for long-form teaching.
- Use Mermaid diagrams only when they improve workflow understanding.

## Lesson quality gate

Before marking a lesson complete, verify:
- Objectives are met by the lesson and assessment.
- Definitions and terminology are consistent.
- Hindi and Thai explanations are meaningful and accurate.
- Examples are internally consistent.
- Procedures identify who does what and when.
- Risk and compliance boundaries are explicit.
- Assignments have clear deliverables and rubrics.
- References are credible and relevant.
- Markdown links and headings are valid.

## Suggested learner workflow

Read → annotate terminology → reproduce the workflow → complete the practical lab → answer the knowledge check → review feedback → save the artifact to a portfolio.
