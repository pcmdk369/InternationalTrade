# Trade Document Control Checklist

Use this checklist to coordinate review. It is not a universal list of mandatory documents; requirements depend on the product, origin, destination, transport mode, contract and applicable rules.

## A. Transaction identity

- [ ] Order reference is consistent.
- [ ] Buyer and seller legal names are consistent.
- [ ] Addresses and contact details are checked.
- [ ] Product description and specifications are consistent.
- [ ] Quantity and units of measure are consistent.
- [ ] Currency and commercial values are consistent.
- [ ] Country of origin information is supported and not assumed.
- [ ] Destination and delivery place are clear.
- [ ] Incoterms® rule, named place/port and edition are stated where agreed.

## B. Commercial documents (as applicable)

- [ ] Purchase order
- [ ] Sales contract
- [ ] Proforma invoice
- [ ] Commercial invoice
- [ ] Packing list
- [ ] Inspection or quality records
- [ ] Certificate of origin, if required or requested
- [ ] Product permits/certificates, if required

## C. Transport and insurance (as applicable)

- [ ] Booking confirmation
- [ ] Shipping instructions
- [ ] Draft transport document checked
- [ ] Final bill of lading / sea waybill / air waybill
- [ ] Cargo insurance evidence, where arranged
- [ ] Package count, gross/net weight and dimensions checked
- [ ] Marks and numbers checked

## D. Customs and regulatory coordination

- [ ] Product classification reviewed by competent party.
- [ ] Customs valuation information is complete.
- [ ] Origin claim and supporting evidence reviewed.
- [ ] Import/export permits and restrictions checked.
- [ ] Duties and taxes verified with current official sources.
- [ ] Declarations handled by authorized party where required.
- [ ] Release evidence retained.

## E. Quality and release controls

- [ ] Version and issue date are recorded.
- [ ] Amendments are authorized.
- [ ] Required signatures/stamps are verified where applicable.
- [ ] No blank or contradictory material fields remain.
- [ ] Reviewer and approval are recorded.
- [ ] Final copies are stored in controlled location.

## Stop and escalate

Stop document release and escalate if parties, values, quantities, product description, origin, destination or bank instructions conflict; if an alert or restriction appears; or if a document appears altered or unsupported. Never fabricate, backdate or conceal information.
