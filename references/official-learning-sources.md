# Authoritative International Trade Learning Sources

Use these sources for study and verify the current edition, jurisdiction and effective date before applying guidance to a real shipment.

| Organization | Subject | Website |
|---|---|---|
| World Trade Organization (WTO) | Trade rules, agreements and statistics | https://www.wto.org/ |
| World Customs Organization (WCO) | Harmonized System and customs standards | https://www.wcoomd.org/ |
| International Chamber of Commerce (ICC) | Incoterms® and trade rules | https://iccwbo.org/ |
| UN Trade and Development (UNCTAD) | Trade, transport and development research | https://unctad.org/ |
| International Trade Centre (ITC) | SME trade tools and market intelligence | https://www.intracen.org/ |

## Research method

1. Identify the exact product, transaction date, origin and destination.
2. Start with the official authority for the relevant jurisdiction.
3. Check the publication date and effective date.
4. Distinguish binding law from explanatory guidance.
5. Record the source, access date and reviewer.
6. Escalate uncertain legal, tariff, tax, origin or licensing questions to qualified professionals.

## Important cautions

- HS classification depends on detailed product characteristics and the applicable national tariff schedule.
- Duty and tax rates can change and may depend on origin, valuation, preference claims and other conditions.
- A certificate is not automatically required for every shipment.
- Incoterms® rules should be used with the correct edition and named place/port.
- Do not treat a search result, supplier statement or AI answer as final regulatory authority.
