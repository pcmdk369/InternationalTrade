# SOP-ITC-001 — New Trade Order Management

**Version:** 1.0  
**Owner:** Trade Operations  
**Applies to:** New international trade inquiries and orders  
**Status:** Training SOP; adapt and approve before operational use.

## 1. Purpose

Ensure each inquiry is recorded, reviewed, approved and handed into controlled order management.

## 2. Roles

- Coordinator: records information, tracks tasks and communicates approved updates.
- Sales/commercial owner: approves commercial offer and negotiation limits.
- Compliance specialist: reviews restricted goods, parties, sanctions and regulatory questions.
- Finance: verifies payment terms and receipt.
- Operations/logistics: validates shipment plan and execution.
- Manager: approves exceptions according to company authority.

## 3. Procedure

1. Receive inquiry through an approved channel.
2. Create a unique inquiry reference.
3. Record legal names and contact details of buyer and seller.
4. Record product description, specifications, quantity, value, currency and intended use.
5. Record origin, destination, requested delivery date and proposed transport mode.
6. Identify potential permits, product standards, sanctions or restricted-party checks; route to authorized reviewers.
7. Request supplier and freight quotations using comparable assumptions.
8. Prepare a cost estimate and clearly label exclusions and assumptions.
9. Submit the quotation for required approval.
10. Send only the approved quotation.
11. Record buyer acceptance and route contract/PO for authorized review.
12. Open the order and shipment records.
13. Assign owners, deadlines and milestone reminders.
14. Monitor progress and escalate exceptions.
15. Close the order only after delivery, financial reconciliation and record filing.

## 4. Required records

Inquiry form, due-diligence evidence, product specification, RFQ, quotations, approval record, PO/contract, payment status, shipment tracker, document checklist, delivery evidence and closure report.

## 5. Escalation triggers

- Incomplete or inconsistent party/product information.
- Unclear product classification or licensing.
- Sanctions or restricted-party alert.
- Unexpected bank-account change.
- Unapproved price or contract change.
- Missing or inconsistent shipping documents.
- Customs hold, cargo damage, loss or material delay.
- Suspected fraud, bribery or falsified evidence.

## 6. Quality checks

A second authorized reviewer checks critical commercial and document data before release. The coordinator records corrections, reviewer, date and version. Never sign or certify a document outside delegated authority.
