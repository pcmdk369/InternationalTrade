# ITC-03-LES-02 — RFQ and Specification Control

**Level:** Intermediate | **Study time:** 90 minutes

## 1. Learning outcomes
Prepare a complete Request for Quotation (RFQ), control specification revisions and issue the same comparable information to all invited suppliers.

## 2. Definition
An **RFQ** is a structured request asking a supplier to quote against defined requirements, including product, quantity, delivery, commercial and response conditions.

**हिंदी:** RFQ यानी Request for Quotation—निर्धारित specification और शर्तों के आधार पर supplier से कीमत और delivery offer माँगना।  
**ไทย:** RFQ คือคำขอใบเสนอราคาที่ระบุข้อกำหนดสินค้า ปริมาณ การส่งมอบ และเงื่อนไขทางการค้า

## 3. RFQ structure
1. RFQ ID, issue date and closing date.
2. Buyer legal identity and contact.
3. Product specification, model, drawing and revision.
4. Quantity, unit and order pattern.
5. Quality standard, tests, tolerances and acceptance criteria.
6. Packaging, marks and labelling.
7. Delivery destination, target date and delivery rule.
8. Currency, price basis and payment proposal.
9. Required certificates, manuals and technical documents.
10. Warranty, after-sales support and spare parts.
11. Supplier capacity, production lead time and validity.
12. Quotation format and contact for clarification.

## 4. Specification hierarchy
Use an approved master specification. Reference exact revision and date. Attach drawings and data sheets. Identify mandatory requirements separately from preferences. Record deviations in a deviation schedule; never bury exceptions in email text.

## 5. Fair supplier communication
Issue the same baseline specification and deadline to comparable suppliers. Share clarifications consistently where fairness or confidentiality obligations require. Do not disclose one supplier's confidential pricing or proprietary design to another.

## 7. Change control
A specification change after RFQ issue must include reason, impact on price/lead time/compliance, approver, affected suppliers, revised version and acknowledgement. Withdraw obsolete files from active use while preserving the audit record.

## 8. Practical exercise
Prepare an RFQ for 250 stainless-steel water pumps. Do not invent engineering data: mark missing flow rate, head, voltage, material grade, connection size and certification as buyer clarification items.

## 9. Knowledge check
1. Why issue a revision number? **To ensure every party quotes the same controlled requirement.**
2. What is a deviation schedule? **A list of supplier exceptions against the buyer's specification.**
3. Can a coordinator approve a technical change without authority? **No.**
