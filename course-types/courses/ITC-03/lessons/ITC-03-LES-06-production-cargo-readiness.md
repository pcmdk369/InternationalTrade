# ITC-03-LES-06 — Production, Inspection and Cargo Readiness

**Level:** Intermediate | **Study time:** 90 minutes

## 1. Learning outcomes
Track supplier production, monitor quality milestones, prepare inspections and confirm cargo readiness with evidence.

## 2. Production milestones
Order accepted → material procurement → production start → in-process quality check → final inspection → packing → cargo-ready notification → pickup/hand-over. Adapt milestones to product complexity and contract.

## 3. Production tracker
PO; item; quantity; planned/actual start; planned/actual completion; material status; production percentage (define method); quality hold; inspection date/result; packing status; cargo-ready date; owner; evidence; next action.

Do not treat a supplier's percentage estimate as verified output unless the reporting method and evidence are clear.

## 4. Inspection planning
Define inspection scope, sampling plan, acceptance criteria, test method, location, timing, inspector competence and report format in advance. Distinguish pre-production, during-production and pre-shipment inspections. A passed sample does not guarantee every unit is defect-free.

## 5. Cargo-ready checks
- Product and quantity match approved PO.
- Model, serial/batch and labels are correct.
- Packaging protects goods for the route and handling.
- Carton/pallet count, dimensions and gross/net weight are recorded.
- Marks and handling instructions are legible.
- Required manuals, certificates and test reports are available.
- Dangerous goods or special handling declarations are reviewed by competent personnel where applicable.
- Pickup window, location and loading contact are confirmed.

## 6. Hold and release
If inspection fails, quantity differs or documents are incomplete, record nonconformity, quarantine/hold where appropriate, identify corrective action and obtain authorised release. Do not ship merely to protect a schedule.

## 7. Practice
Build a 4-week production milestone plan and inspection checklist for a 200-unit order. Add three exception triggers and escalation owners.

## 8. Knowledge check
1. Does cargo-ready mean customs-cleared? **No.**
2. Why define inspection criteria before inspection? **To ensure objective, agreed acceptance.**
3. What if inspection fails? **Hold affected goods, document nonconformity and follow approved corrective/release process.**
