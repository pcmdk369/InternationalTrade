# ITC-03-LES-07 — Shipment Execution, Delivery and Order Closure

**Level:** Intermediate | **Study time:** 90 minutes

## 1. Learning outcomes
Coordinate handover and shipment milestones, manage exceptions, verify delivery and close the order with financial and document reconciliation.

## 2. Shipment execution checklist
Confirm booking; cargo pickup; export process status; transport document draft review; departure evidence; ETA and transshipment updates; destination arrival; import clearance owner/status; delivery appointment; proof of delivery; damage/shortage notation.

The coordinator tracks and escalates; authorised parties perform regulated declarations and transport operations.

## 3. Milestone definitions
- **Cargo ready:** Supplier declares cargo available for pickup.
- **ETD:** Estimated time of departure.
- **ATD:** Actual time of departure.
- **ETA:** Estimated time of arrival.
- **ATA:** Actual time of arrival.
- **POD:** Proof of delivery, evidence that delivery occurred under the relevant process.

Record time zone and source for each timestamp. ETA is an estimate, not a guarantee.

## 4. Exception workflow
Log issue → assess impact → contain → assign owner → set deadline → communicate → implement corrective action → verify evidence → close. Examples include delay, rollover, customs hold, missing document, damage, shortage or payment discrepancy.

## 5. Delivery acceptance
Compare delivered quantity and condition with PO and packing list. Record visible damage and shortage promptly on delivery records, take photographs where safe, notify relevant parties within contract/policy deadlines and preserve packaging/evidence as instructed. Do not sign a clean receipt if the goods are visibly damaged or short; follow local receiving procedure.

## 6. Financial close
Reconcile PO, supplier invoice, goods receipt, freight/other invoices and approved variations. Check currency, tax, credit notes, advance payments and bank evidence. Investigate variances before closure.

## 7. Order closure pack
Final PO/revisions; contract; approved quote; invoice; packing list; transport document; customs records as applicable; permits/certificates; inspection; insurance; delivery evidence; claims; payment reconciliation; lessons learned; retention classification.

## 8. Performance review
Measure on-time delivery against agreed baseline, cost variance, supplier defect rate, document error rate, exception response time and claim resolution. Define formulas and data sources before comparing suppliers.

## 9. Knowledge check
1. Is ETA the actual arrival? **No.**
2. What is POD? **Proof of delivery.**
3. Why reconcile PO, invoice and receipt? **To detect price, quantity and acceptance mismatches.**
