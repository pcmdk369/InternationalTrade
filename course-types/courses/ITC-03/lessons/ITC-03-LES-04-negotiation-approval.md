# ITC-03-LES-04 — Negotiation and Commercial Approval

**Level:** Intermediate | **Study time:** 75–90 minutes

## 1. Learning outcomes
Prepare a negotiation plan, separate interests from positions, manage concessions and stay within delegated approval authority.

## 2. Preparation
Define target outcome, walk-away conditions, best alternative, must-have requirements, negotiable items, evidence, authority limit and approval path. Know total landed-cost assumptions and supplier constraints before discussing price.

## 3. Negotiable dimensions
Price; volume; delivery schedule; payment milestones; currency; validity; quality tolerances; inspection; packaging; warranty; spare parts; service; exclusivity; forecast commitments; remedies and dispute terms.

A lower price may be exchanged for higher volume, longer forecast visibility or different payment timing. Quantify the impact rather than making casual promises.

## 4. Negotiation method
1. Set agenda and confirm shared facts.
2. Ask questions about supplier capacity and constraints.
3. Separate mandatory requirements from preferences.
4. Offer conditional trades (“if/then”) rather than unilateral concessions.
5. Summarise every agreed point.
6. Record open issues and owner/deadline.
7. Obtain internal approval before committing.
8. Confirm final terms in the authorised contract/PO.

## 5. Authority and ethics
Never promise a discount, exclusivity, payment date or technical waiver outside authority. Do not offer bribes, undisclosed commissions or misleading forecasts. Keep confidential information protected.

## 6. Worked example
Buyer requests a 5% price reduction. Supplier cannot reduce unit price but offers consolidated monthly shipments that reduce freight per unit. Coordinator models the total cost and delivery risk, submits options to the manager and records approval before confirming.

## 7. Negotiation record
Date; participants; issue; buyer position; supplier position; evidence; options; concessions offered/received; decision; approver; open actions; next meeting.

## 8. Knowledge check
1. What is a conditional concession? **A concession tied to a reciprocal commitment.**
2. Can a coordinator bind the company without delegation? **No.**
3. Why record negotiation outcomes? **To prevent later disagreement and preserve approval evidence.**
