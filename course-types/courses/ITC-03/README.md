# ITC-03 — Import & Export Operations

**Phase:** I — Foundations | **Level:** Intermediate | **Suggested duration:** 1 week  
**Languages:** English, Hindi and Thai

## Purpose
Teach the operational sequence from customer inquiry and supplier quotation through order confirmation, production, shipment, delivery, payment reconciliation and closure.

## Learning outcomes
Prepare and process an inquiry/RFQ; evaluate quotations; coordinate negotiation and approvals; control purchase orders and order acknowledgements; monitor production and cargo readiness; coordinate shipment milestones; manage exceptions; close and archive an order.
