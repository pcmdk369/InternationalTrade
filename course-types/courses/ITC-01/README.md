# ITC-01 — International Trade Fundamentals

**Phase:** I — Foundations  
**Level:** Intermediate foundation / bridge to advanced practice  
**Suggested duration:** Week 1  
**Languages:** English, Hindi and Thai

## Course purpose

Build a reliable conceptual model of international trade before studying documentation, customs, logistics, finance and compliance. Learners will understand what crosses borders, who participates, why trade occurs, and how a transaction moves from inquiry to delivery.

## Learning outcomes

By the end of ITC-01, learners should be able to:

1. Define international trade accurately.
2. Distinguish domestic trade from cross-border trade.
3. Explain imports, exports, re-exports and transit trade.
4. Identify the major parties in a trade transaction.
5. Describe common reasons countries and firms trade.
6. Map a basic import/export transaction lifecycle.
7. Use foundational English, Hindi and Thai terminology.
8. Identify risks that require specialist review.

## Required practical work

- Draw a cross-border buyer–seller transaction map.
- Build a three-language glossary of at least 25 terms.
- Classify example transactions as domestic, import, export, re-export or transit.
- Prepare a one-page coordinator responsibility map.

## Assessment

- Knowledge check: 20%
- Terminology workbook: 20%
- Transaction map: 25%
- Practical case analysis: 35%

## Completion criteria

Complete all eight lessons and practical work, score at least 70%, and demonstrate correct escalation of customs, regulatory and payment questions.
