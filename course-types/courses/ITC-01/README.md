# ITC-01 — International Trade Fundamentals

**Phase:** I — Foundations  
**Level:** Intermediate foundation / bridge to advanced practice  
**Suggested duration:** 1–2 weeks  
**Languages:** English, Hindi and Thai

## Course purpose
Build a reliable conceptual model of international trade before studying documentation, customs, logistics, finance and compliance. Learners understand what crosses borders, who participates, why trade occurs, and how a transaction moves from inquiry to delivery.

## Learning outcomes
Learners should be able to define international trade; distinguish domestic and cross-border trade; explain import, export, re-export and transit; identify transaction parties; explain comparative advantage and landed cost; map a transaction lifecycle; use foundational trilingual terminology; and escalate legal, regulatory, payment and safety risks.

## Required practical work
- Draw a cross-border buyer–seller transaction map.
- Build a three-language glossary of at least 25 terms.
- Classify example transactions as domestic, import, export, re-export or transit.
- Prepare a coordinator responsibility map and RACI.
- Build a milestone tracker and risk register.
- Complete the integrated case and knowledge assessment.

## Assessment
- Knowledge quiz: 20%
- Three-language glossary: 20%
- Transaction and stakeholder map: 25%
- Integrated case analysis: 35%

**Pass standard:** 70% overall, with no critical failure on compliance, safety or fraud escalation.
