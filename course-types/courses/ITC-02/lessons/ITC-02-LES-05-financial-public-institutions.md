# ITC-02-LES-05 — Banks, Insurers and Public Institutions

**Level:** Intermediate | **Study time:** 90 minutes | **Languages:** English, Hindi, Thai

## 1. Learning outcomes
Explain the roles and limits of banks, insurers, export credit agencies, customs, standards bodies, chambers and international organisations.

## 2. Financial institutions
**Commercial banks** provide account services, payment transfers, foreign exchange, documentary collections, letters of credit and financing subject to their policies and applicable rules.

**Issuing bank** issues a documentary credit at the applicant's request. **Advising bank** authenticates and advises the credit without necessarily adding its own payment undertaking. **Confirming bank** adds its confirmation when it agrees to do so. Roles and obligations depend on the credit and applicable rules.

Banks examine documents under the credit terms and applicable banking rules; they do not ordinarily inspect the actual goods or guarantee that goods match the buyer's expectations.

**Export credit agencies (ECAs)** may support exports through insurance, guarantees or financing, subject to mandate and eligibility.

## 3. Insurance providers
Cargo insurance responds only to covered risks under the policy wording, insured value, exclusions, warranties, conditions and claims procedure. Review:
- Insured party and insurable interest.
- Cargo description, route and conveyance.
- Insured value and valuation basis.
- Covered risks and exclusions.
- Deductible/excess.
- Packaging and security conditions.
- Notice and claim deadlines.
- Required survey and evidence.

Insurance does not replace good packaging, carrier selection or incident controls.

## 4. Public authorities
- **Customs authority:** Administers customs law, declarations, duties and border controls.
- **Product regulator:** Oversees specified product safety, health, technical or market rules.
- **Licensing authority:** Issues or administers permissions for controlled activities/goods.
- **Tax authority:** Administers applicable tax rules.
- **Port/transport authority:** Regulates or manages specified infrastructure and transport matters.
- **Standards body:** Develops or publishes standards; a standards body is not always the regulator that makes a standard mandatory.

Identify the competent authority for the specific product and jurisdiction.

## 5. Chambers and international institutions
- **Chambers of commerce:** Business networks, trade information, events and in some jurisdictions certain document/certificate services under authority.
- **WTO:** Provides a multilateral framework for trade rules among members; it is not an importer/exporter's customs broker.
- **WCO:** Supports customs cooperation and development of customs instruments and standards.
- **ICC:** Publishes commercial rules and guidance, including Incoterms® rules and documentary-credit rules.
- **UNCTAD:** Research, analysis and technical cooperation on trade and development.
- **ITC (International Trade Centre):** Supports small businesses and trade development.
Institutional mandates and services should be checked against their official sources.

## 6. Authority boundaries
A bank cannot grant an import permit. A chamber cannot override customs law. A forwarder cannot guarantee that a regulator will approve a product. A certificate issuer can only certify within its authority and scope. The importer/exporter remains responsible for ensuring declarations and information are accurate under applicable law.

## 7. Practical assignment
Create an institutional source register for one product and route. Record authority name, jurisdiction, subject, official URL, rule/permit, last checked date, responsible reviewer and unresolved question. Distinguish official legal sources from commercial commentary.

## 8. Knowledge check
1. Does a bank inspecting documents physically inspect cargo? **No.**
2. Does cargo insurance cover all delays? **Not automatically; check policy terms and exclusions.**
3. Is WTO a customs authority? **No.**
4. Who confirms product-specific import requirements? **The competent authority and qualified local compliance/customs specialists.**
