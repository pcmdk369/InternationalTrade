# ITC-02 — Global Trade Ecosystem

**Phase:** I — Foundations | **Level:** Intermediate | **Suggested duration:** 1 week  
**Languages:** English, Hindi and Thai

## Purpose
Understand the organisations, commercial models, service providers and public institutions that make international trade possible. Learn to map each party's role without confusing commercial ownership, physical custody, transport, customs authority or payment responsibility.

## Learning outcomes
By the end, learners can:
- Map the main participants in a cross-border transaction.
- Distinguish manufacturers, traders, distributors, agents and brokers.
- Explain the roles of banks, insurers, carriers, forwarders, ports and customs brokers.
- Describe the purpose of chambers, regulators and international institutions.
- Compare B2B, B2C, D2C and marketplace models.
- Prepare an authority boundary and responsibility matrix.
