## About this repository

A structured academic and practical training program for International Trade Coordinators. It develops the knowledge and operational discipline needed to coordinate international buying and selling, supplier communication, commercial documentation, freight, customs interfaces, payments, compliance, reporting and delivery.

**This repository is a learning resource, not legal, tax, customs-broker, banking or accredited qualification advice.** Actual transactions must be checked against current official rules, contracts, bank requirements and qualified professionals in each jurisdiction.

## Learning outcomes

Graduates of this learning program should be able to:

1. Map an international trade transaction and identify stakeholder responsibilities.
2. Coordinate buyer inquiries, sourcing, RFQs, quotations, purchase orders and sales contracts.
3. Prepare and cross-check common commercial and transport documents.
4. Coordinate freight, shipment milestones, customs brokers and final delivery.
5. Understand Incoterms® 2020, payment methods, trade finance concepts and cost build-ups.
6. Maintain supplier, order, shipment, risk and document registers.
7. Use spreadsheets, ERP/CRM concepts and reporting tools.
8. Communicate professionally in English, Hindi and Thai.
9. Escalate compliance, fraud, safety, quality and delivery risks appropriately.
10. Produce clear operational and management reports.

## Academic standard

Each full lesson should include: learning outcomes, prerequisites, terminology, accurate definitions, conceptual explanation, worked examples, step-by-step workflow, practical exercise, common errors, knowledge check, answer key or rubric, and references. Language support should clarify meaning rather than replace precise English trade terminology.

## Responsible use

- Verify current laws, duties, product controls, sanctions and licensing with official authorities.
- Never fabricate certificates, invoices, declarations, inspection results or shipping records.
- Protect personal, commercial and financial information.
- Independently verify bank-account changes using a trusted known contact.
- Do not treat AI-generated classification, customs or legal conclusions as authoritative.
- Incoterms® rules do not by themselves determine ownership/title transfer or payment terms.
